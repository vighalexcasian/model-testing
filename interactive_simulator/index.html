Weekly MACD (12, 26, 9) Buy and hold

Historical, adjusted SPY daily candles bundled with this page. The strategy calculates the MACD(12, 26, 9) histogram from completed weekly closes: positive readings hold a long position and negative readings hold a short position. Each weekly signal takes effect at the next daily open, with returns measured between daily opens. Reversals are charged for both sides. The simulation excludes margin interest, stock-borrow fees, taxes, and additional slippage, and is not investment advice.
